# Backlink and partnership outreach kit

The links that count come from organisations that genuinely know Mshwar: its partners, its
hub, universities, and places it lists. This kit gives the assets, the targets and the
messages. Send from a real address on the domain (for example `hello@mshwarlb.com` via
Cloudflare Email Routing) and log every contact in `outreach-tracker.csv`.

Never buy links, join link exchanges or send AI-generated guest posts; one penalty costs more
than a year of outreach.

## Assets to point people to

| Asset                | URL                           | Why someone links to it                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Lebanon travel guide | `/lebanon`                    | A neutral, useful resource for any site about Lebanon |
| Things to do by kind | `/things-to-do/{nature…food}` | Hiking clubs, food bloggers, hotels                   |
| Destination pages    | `/destinations/{slug}`        | Municipalities, guesthouses, local businesses         |
| Place pages          | `/experiences/{slug}`         | The place itself ("find us on Mshwar")                |
| Partner badge        | `/partners`                   | Copy-paste code for listed places, guides and drivers |
| About                | `/about`                      | Press, hubs and universities describing the project   |

## Targets, in order of value

1. **UNRWA Digital Hub** — the programme Mshwar was built with. Ask for a project or alumni
   showcase page, a news post, or a mention in a graduation/demo-day write-up, linking to
   `https://mshwarlb.com`.
2. **Universities** — your own university first (project showcase, student news, faculty page),
   then tourism and hospitality faculties (for example the Lebanese University's tourism
   faculty) and computer science departments. Offer: a guest talk, a student data or
   usability project, or a free account for a tourism class.
3. **Places, guides and drivers listed on Mshwar** — each one is a natural link to its own page
   via the `/partners` badge. Start with the places that have a website.
4. **Municipalities and local tourism offices** — "Visit {town}" pages often list resources;
   offer the town's Mshwar destination page.
5. **Guesthouses and hotels** — a "plan your day around us" link for their guests.
6. **Hiking clubs and nature reserves** — link to `/things-to-do/nature` and destination pages.
7. **Lebanese travel bloggers and Instagram creators** — offer the planner for their followers'
   itineraries; ask for a link in blog posts, not paid placement.
8. **Diaspora organisations and media** — "first trip back" guides before summer and Christmas.

## Email templates

Replace everything in {braces}. Keep each email short and specific; one ask per email.

### UNRWA Digital Hub (English)

> Subject: Mshwar — the Lebanon trip planner I built at the Digital Hub is live
>
> Hi {name},
>
> Mshwar, the project I built during {programme/cohort} at the UNRWA Digital Hub, is now live at
> https://mshwarlb.com. It helps travellers and residents discover real places across Lebanon
> and plan a day around them — in English, Arabic and French.
>
> Would the Hub be open to featuring it on {the alumni/projects page / a news post}? A short
> description and a link to https://mshwarlb.com would help a lot as the project starts out.
> I'm happy to send photos, a demo video or a short write-up.
>
> Thank you for the support during the programme,
> {your name}

## Follow-up rules

- One follow-up after 7–10 days, then stop.
- Say thank you publicly when someone links (a post tagging them) — it earns the next link.
- Check new links monthly in Search Console → Links, and note them in the tracker.
